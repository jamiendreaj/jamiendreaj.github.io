Short story

I came to the United States alone at 24, by way of Albania and Italy, speaking four languages and carrying one belief: you can build the life you want if you learn fast and outwork the room.

For nine years I did exactly that in real estate — closing high-stakes deals across New York and Las Vegas as both agent and team leader. Reading people, spotting the detail everyone else missed, and staying calm when a lot of money was on the line. Those are security instincts before they're sales instincts.

Then I taught myself to build. Not by going back to school, but by actually shipping — three iOS apps through the full App Store pipeline, and a live e-commerce operation running on automation I wrote myself. I learned to understand systems from the inside: how they're made, and how they break. In 2025 I went deeper still, earning the Google Cybersecurity Certificate and a stack of security and IT credentials.

I'm looking for a role in New York where I can build, solve real problems, and keep growing — on a team that values someone hands-on, resourceful, and relentless about finishing what she starts.

Experience

Dogma Systems Internship

Software Support Intern · December 2025 – May 2026 · Italy

Six months at a software company building fleet management systems for car rental businesses. I worked incoming tickets from the clients running it, resolving issues in real time: reading production logs to trace why something had failed, diagnosing errors live while a customer was blocked, and writing SQL against PostgreSQL to investigate problems and import data. I sat between the engineering team and the clients, handling issue tracking, sprint coordination and bug reporting in Jira. Ticket queues, Slack, bug reports — the ordinary machinery of a working engineering team. It was my first time inside a production system with real customers depending on it, and the clearest lesson was the distance between how software is designed and how people actually use it. I used Claude daily throughout, to move faster and to reason through problems I hadn’t met before. Written reference from the CEO available on request.
